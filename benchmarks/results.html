Improvement guidance

  • Keep default batching and overlap only independent pools.
  • Keep fresh inventory scoped to configured roots and pools; optimize native reads before adding a cache.
  • Recursive destroy needs proof that every descendant candidate is safe.
  • Keep channel programs optional unless enormous real prune sets justify root-only Lua and partial-application complexity.
  • Retest on physical pools before device tuning.

Method

Snapshot runs create one snapshot per dataset. Prune runs seed 6 expired snapshots per dataset; zsnap seeds carry org.zsnap:managed=yes. All outcomes are verified. A separate warm plan-only pass and its three native inventory commands are timed on the same seeded state.
